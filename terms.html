Terms of use

Daybell Reminders is a personal Windows reminder utility, provided for accounts and computers you are authorized to use. There is no subscription, payment service, or developer-operated Google data service.

Authorization and control

Google access requires your own interactive approval of the documented read-only permissions. You can pause the application, quit it, disable startup, uninstall it, or revoke Google access. Google, Windows, and browser services continue to have their own applicable terms. The application and website are independent of Google, Mozilla, and Microsoft.

Reminder limitations

Delivery depends on a healthy signed-in Windows session, an awake computer, Windows notification preferences, valid authorization, synchronized source data, and working operating-system components. Offline operation can use previously cached reminders, but cannot know changes that have not synchronized. Reminders are a convenience and should not be your sole method for time-critical obligations.

Google Tasks scheduled times, recurrence rules, and Google notification preferences are not available through the public Tasks API. Configured local times are local settings, not synchronized Google settings. The setup guide explains catch-up, all-day-event, timezone, and daylight-saving policies. No absolute delivery-time or visible-notification guarantee is made.

Data and maintenance

The privacy policy describes access, storage, external connections and deletion. Review updated application sources and accompanying third-party licenses when maintaining an installation. Keep Windows and runtime dependencies supported and updated.
