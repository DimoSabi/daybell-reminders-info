Privacy policy

This policy describes the Daybell Reminders Windows application and this informational website. Daybell is a personal reminder utility. Its maintainer does not operate a service that receives your Google Calendar or Google Tasks contents.

Google information accessed

With your consent, the application reads calendar identifiers, titles, timezones and default reminders; selected event identifiers, titles, links, dates, recurrence instances, reminder overrides and cancellation status; and whether you have declined an event. It reads task-list identifiers and titles, and selected task identifiers, titles, links, due dates, and completion, deletion or hidden status. It checks the primary-calendar account against the account configured for the installation.

The application requests only calendar.events.readonly, calendar.calendarlist.readonly, and tasks.readonly. It does not request your Google password, read your browser cookies, or request write access. It does not retrieve event descriptions, locations, guest email lists, task notes, or attachments for reminder calculation.

Use and storage

The information is used to synchronize scheduling changes, calculate reminders, display native Windows notifications, open Google item links, and prevent repeated submissions. A minimal SQLite cache, reminder ledger, settings and operational diagnostics are stored on the laptop. Access and refresh tokens and the desktop client configuration are encrypted with Windows DPAPI for the current Windows account. The scheduling cache itself is protected by file permissions rather than DPAPI encryption.

Notification titles are generic by default. A user can enable private titles locally; Windows controls notification and lock-screen visibility. Timing and item details can also appear in the application's local management interface.

Sharing and external connections

Authorization requests go to Google's authorization service, tokens are exchanged with Google's token service, and authorized reads go to Google's official Calendar and Tasks APIs. Windows receives the notification content, and clicking a notification opens a Google HTTPS link in the user's browser. Daybell does not sell data, use advertising, send Google contents to its maintainer, share data with a developer-operated backend, or use Google data to train AI models. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This static website does not access your Google account and contains no sign-in form, analytics, external fonts, or tracking scripts. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web requests, including IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. If hosted on GitHub Pages, see GitHub's privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

The local source cache is refreshed as synchronization proceeds. Obsolete records and deleted-item markers may remain until the next full refresh. Terminal ledger entries are pruned after approximately 90 days; operational logs rotate at approximately one MiB plus one previous log. Settings and protected credentials remain until replaced or removed by the user.

You can pause or quit the application, disable startup, remove local state with the provided maintenance controls, or uninstall with the RemoveData option to remove the installed application and its cache, settings and protected credentials. Backups made by you are separate. Uninstalling does not delete Google events or tasks. You can revoke Daybell access independently through Google Account connections.

Contact and changes

The personal project maintainer is DimoSabi on GitHub. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, or private calendar contents in public reports. Material changes in data handling should be reflected here before the changed behavior is used.
